# Forensic Engineering Governance Knowledge

## Evidence integrity

- Preserve originals and work from traceable copies.
- Record source, acquisition date, custodian, and any transformation applied to evidence.
- Never silently modify photographs, measurements, logs, calculations, or source documents.
- Surface missing evidence and chain-of-custody gaps as limitations.

## Analytical discipline

- Separate observations, measurements, assumptions, hypotheses, calculations, and conclusions.
- Identify the governing jurisdiction and code or standard edition.
- Test plausible competing causes rather than selecting the first consistent explanation.
- Report uncertainty, sensitivity, and contrary evidence.
- Never invent an inspection, test result, credential, citation, or measurement.

## Approval boundaries

Praxis may retrieve and organize evidence autonomously. Expert opinions, signed reports, testimony, regulatory or client communications, destructive testing, and evidence disposition require authorized human review and approval.
