# Praxis Homeschool v1 knowledge

This knowledge is grounding for the parent-operated `homeschool` pack across **FL, GA, SC, TN, VA, WV, MD, PA, OH, NJ, NY, CT, and MA**. It is product guidance, not legal advice. Always use the source-versioned `HomeschoolProfile`, parent-confirmed route, current agency materials, and local instructions before labeling a filing ready.

## Non-negotiable operating boundary

- The parent/guardian is the accountable home educator.
- Praxis compares routes but never chooses the family's legal status.
- READ and DRAFT may run autonomously. SEND and DESTRUCTIVE are held.
- The parent verifies, attests, signs, files, releases, purchases, and submits.
- Form completion is not proof of legal compliance or external acceptance.
- Never fabricate attendance, hours, grades, credits, evidence, assessment results, receipts, signatures, evaluator conclusions, or approvals.
- Public virtual/cyber school enrollment is not independent homeschool when the public school remains school of record.

## State route and compliance matrix

### FL — Florida

Independent home education: notice within 30 days; contemporaneous educational activity/reading log and representative work; portfolio retained two years and inspectable on 15 days' written notice; annual evaluation through an accepted statutory method; termination notice plus evaluation within 30 days. A private-school umbrella is the school of record and does not inherit the independent notice, portfolio, or evaluation checklist. PEP Scholarship workflows use scholarship-organization rules and accepted annual testing, not the independent-home-education filing path. Do not treat either route as public virtual enrollment.

Primary source: Fla. Stat. §1002.41.

### GA — Georgia

Home study: Declaration of Intent within 30 days and annually by September 1; parent/tutor high-school diploma or equivalent; 180 days × 4.5 hours; reading, language arts, mathematics, social studies, science; nationally standardized testing at least every three years beginning after grade 3; annual individualized progress report retained at least three years.

Primary source: O.C.G.A. §§20-2-690 and 20-2-690.1; Georgia DOE July 2025 guidance.

### SC — South Carolina

Route must be explicit:

1. Option 1 district approval — 180 days × 4.5 hours, records, progress, testing and district process.
2. Option 2 SCAIHS — association standing plus statutory minimums.
3. Option 3 qualifying association — association of at least 50 members, 180 days, subjects, plan/diary, portfolio, attendance and semiannual progress.

Do not merge the options.

Primary source: S.C. Code §§59-65-40, -45, -47.

### TN — Tennessee

Tennessee recognizes two home-school routes. Under **independent home school**, the parent/legal guardian is teacher of record, must hold a high-school diploma or approved equivalency, files an Intent to Home School with the resident district, renews before each subsequent school year, provides 180 days × 4 hours, submits year-end attendance, and arranges TCAP in grades 5, 7 and 9. The parent creates grades, transcript, and diploma. Under a **church-related umbrella school**, no Intent to Home School is filed; the supervising school sets parent-teacher requirements, owns recordkeeping/testing, and issues transcript and diploma. Proof of umbrella enrollment may be needed when withdrawing from public school. An accredited online school is not Tennessee homeschool merely because instruction occurs at home.

Primary sources: Tenn. Code Ann. §§49-6-3050 and 49-50-801; Tennessee Department of Education Home School page and *Independent Home School Requirements* (October 2023), verified July 18, 2026.

### VA — Virginia

Home instruction under §22.1-254.1: one of four parent qualification paths; annual notice and subject list by August 15; midyear compliance process; evidence of progress by August 1 through qualifying test/evaluator/equivalent evidence. Religious exemption under §22.1-254(B) is separate and must not inherit ordinary home-instruction paperwork automatically.

### WV — West Virginia

Distinct classifications include board-approved instruction, notice home instruction, Hope Scholarship individualized instruction, learning pod, and microschool. The household pack operates the first four only after route confirmation; microschool administration remains a separate product boundary. Notice-route duties—five subjects, provider qualification, annual assessment, three-year assessment retention, and grade 3/5/8/11 submission by June 30—must not be copied onto Hope or pod routes without current route-specific verification.

Primary source: W. Va. Code §18-8-1.

### MD — Maryland

Local supervision or registered nonpublic/church supervision. Initial statement at least 15 days before starting; annual verification; regular/thorough instruction in eight areas. Under local supervision, maintain portfolio and prepare semester reviews at mutually agreeable times, no more than three per year. Deficiency notice starts a correction workflow.

Primary source: COMAR 13A.10.01.

### PA — Pennsylvania

Home education is distinct from private tutor, public cyber charter and private online/umbrella use. The home-education route uses an affidavit or unsworn declaration, supervisor qualification and statutory household conditions, 180 days or 900 elementary / 990 secondary hours, grade-band subjects, portfolio, tests in grades 3, 5 and 8, qualified annual evaluator, and evaluation to the superintendent. The private-tutor route retains instructional time/subject duties but does not inherit the home-education affidavit, portfolio, testing, or evaluator checklist; track the tutor's Pennsylvania certification and annual required filing instead. State-recognized diploma status depends on statutory issuer/process.

Primary sources: 24 P.S. §§13-1327 and 13-1327.1; PA DOE guide revised May 2026.

### OH — Ohio

Current post-reform home education: notify within five calendar days after beginning, moving, or withdrawing, then annually by August 30; assure instruction in ELA, mathematics, science, history, government and social studies. Do not import stale 900-hour or testing rules. Online community schools are public schools.

Primary source: Ohio Rev. Code §3321.042 and current Ohio DOE guidance.

### NJ — New Jersey

Equivalent instruction elsewhere than at school, compulsory ages 6–16. No universal approval, curriculum review, annual test, or routine monitoring system. Communication can prevent truancy confusion but is not a fictional mandatory filing. District courses, sports, materials and re-entry decisions may be local. Parent record does not automatically become a state-endorsed diploma.

Primary source: N.J.S.A. 18A:38-25 and NJ DOE homeschool FAQ.

### NY — New York

Intent by July 1 or within 14 days for midyear start/move; IHIP; four quarterly reports; 180 days; 900 hours grades 1–6 and 990 hours grades 7–12; grade-band subjects; annual assessment and possible remediation/probation. District correspondence and receipts matter. Home instruction does not itself produce a New York public-school diploma.

Primary source: 8 NYCRR §100.10 and NYSED guidance.

### CT — Connecticut

Statutory equivalent-instruction and subject floor. Agency/C-14 notice, plan, attendance log, portfolio and assessment evidence are prudent recommendations unless a current binding source makes a specific item mandatory. Do not turn guidance into law. Connecticut does not accredit a parent homeschool program; re-entry credit may be local.

Primary source: Conn. Gen. Stat. §10-184 and state guidance.

### MA — Massachusetts

Prior local approval under M.G.L. c.76 §1. District may reasonably review curriculum, instruction time, parent competency, materials and assessment method under *Care & Protection of Charles*. Local forms vary. *Brunelle* limits mandatory home visits as a universal condition. Praxis can prepare a review-ready package but never claim district approval.

## Child privacy and tutoring

- Household identity, custody, health/disability, values, finance and child AI interactions are separate data classes.
- Learners cannot access sibling or restricted parent records.
- Tutor/co-op access is assigned learner + course + fields + expiry only.
- Evaluator receives only the parent-selected evidence packet.
- External disclosures require parent approval and an audit event.
- FERPA is additional context when a covered school or provider participates; it is not the default household legal model.
- No advertising, sale, model training, profiling, biometrics, affective computing, covert attention scoring or public release of child data.
- Tutoring is parent-visible and hint-first. No complete answers on graded/summative work. No autonomous child accounts, purchases, posts, or unknown-adult contact.

## Evidence, assessment, and support

Attendance requires dated parent attestation plus evidence ID. Multiple subjects on one date count as one actual day. Portfolio exports strip arbitrary metadata such as EXIF/GPS. Assessment method comes from state/route and is selected by the parent; Praxis never impersonates an evaluator or test administrator. Parent support plans are not diagnoses or IEPs. District service inquiries are drafts and do not promise eligibility, FAPE, accommodations or services.

## Transcript and funding

Every transcript course needs declared policy, credits, parent-finalized grade, description, and evidence. GPA uses finite Decimal arithmetic. Dual enrollment needs provider identity and official record hash. Credential names the actual issuer; no state-issued/accredited claim without external proof.

Funding is optional and never establishes legal homeschool status. Eligible categories, vendors and dates are source-versioned. Missing or incomplete rules produce **uncertain**, not eligible. Only verified eligible expenses enter a parent-approved packet; Praxis never submits reimbursement claims.
