# FERPA pack

Starter policy. Not legal advice.

Dial: `ferpa`. Default: off.

Detectors look for "education record", "student record", "report card", a student-id label, and email addresses near a student or pupil cue.

Enforce mode asks before those strings leave through the browser, MCP, `web_fetch`, or Telegram, and masks them in tool output and memory. The technical retention window is 365 days.

Citations recorded on the pack: 20 U.S.C. § 1232g and 34 CFR Part 99.
