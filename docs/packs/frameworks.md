# Framework and later dials

These packs ship so every catalog dial has a data file. They are starter notes, not certifications or legal advice. Except for PCI, they do not add detectors. Turning one to enforce without a detector does not change tool or model behavior.

| Pack | Dial | Note |
|---|---|---|
| SOC 2 | `soc2` | Placeholder for operational evidence. Not an attestation. |
| EU AI Act | `eu_ai_act` | Placeholder for disclosure and traceability. |
| CCPA/CPRA | `ccpa` | Placeholder. Access and delete use `praxis-prime gdpr export` and `erase`. |
| PCI DSS | `pci` | Luhn-valid card numbers are denied on egress and redacted to the last four. Not a PCI assessment. |
| NIST AI RMF | `nist_ai_rmf` | Placeholder for Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | `iso_42001` | Placeholder for an AI management-system pack. Not a certification. |

PCI citations on the pack point at the PCI DSS cardholder-data concept. The other packs record their framework names as references only.
