# Classical

Great books and lamplight, in the WisdomForge line. Dark mode is the default: ground `#0a0a0f`, antique gold `#c9a96e`. Display is Fraunces. Body is Praxis Office Sans Subset, a subset of Source Sans 3. Code is Praxis Office Mono Subset, a subset of Source Code Pro. The Socratic prompt style belongs to a pack or a persona, not to this theme.

## Colours

The core colours are the Addendum A §1.7 pairs. `borderStrong` is the muted colour and `ring` is the accent. Both clear 3:1 on the background and on the raised surface, so they were not moved. Light-mode `accentFg` is the raised stone `#fcf8f0`, which clears 4.5:1 on the olive accent. Dark-mode `accentFg` is the ground `#0a0a0f` on the gold.

Light: background `#f5efe3`, raised `#fcf8f0`, text `#221d16`, muted `#5b5041`, accent `#5b6b2f`, accent text `#fcf8f0`. Status: ok `#3f6b2a`, warn `#7d5a00`, danger `#9b2c20`.

Dark: background `#0a0a0f`, raised `#13131a`, text `#f3eadc`, muted `#b5a48c`, accent `#c9a96e`, accent text `#0a0a0f`. Status: ok `#9cc28a`, warn `#d9b25f`, danger `#e57368`.

Contrast level is WCAG 2.2 AA in both modes. Optional tokens are derived by the engine.

## Fonts

Latin subset WOFF2 files from the `ofl/` tree of github.com/google/fonts. SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence text is `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. The package does not load fonts from a network. Fraunces has no Reserved Font Name, so the subset keeps that family name. Praxis Office Sans Subset and Praxis Office Mono Subset are the Legal Office subset files copied here so this package stands alone. Source is a Reserved Font Name, so those copies do not use it.

## Ornaments

`assets/ornaments/capital.svg` is a geometric column capital used as the divider. There is no watermark and no figure.
